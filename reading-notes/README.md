# 📚 Scientific Paper Notes

A structured reading log for neuroscience papers and reviews.

## Paper note template

### Citation
Author(s), year, title, journal.

### Research question
What did the researchers want to understand?

### Methods
How did they investigate it?

### Main findings
What did they discover?

### Important evidence
Which result, figure, or analysis matters most?

### Limitations
What can the study not establish?

### My questions
What do I still not understand?

### Possible follow-up
What new research question does this paper suggest?
