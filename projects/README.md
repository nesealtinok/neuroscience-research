# Projects

Projects turn a neuroscience question into something testable, computational, visual, or otherwise demonstrable.

## Project template

### 1. Question
What am I trying to find out?

### 2. Background
What is already known?

### 3. Hypothesis / objective
What do I expect or aim to demonstrate?

### 4. Method
What will I build, measure, analyze, or compare?

### 5. Data / evidence
What information will I collect or use?

### 6. Analysis
How will I analyze the evidence?

### 7. Results
What happened?

### 8. Limitations
What could make the result unreliable or incomplete?

### 9. Conclusion
What can I reasonably conclude?

### 10. Next question
What should be investigated next?

## Project ideas

- Reaction-time measurement and analysis
- Computational analysis of an open neuroscience dataset
- Brain-region visualization
- A small AI/ML project using behavioral or neuroscience data

These are starting ideas, not commitments. A project should begin with a clear question.
