# 🧠 Brain Bee

A dedicated space for Brain Bee preparation and neuroscience knowledge development.

## Goals

- Build a strong foundation in neuroscience
- Identify weak areas and review them systematically
- Practice scientific terminology in English
- Track practice results over time
- Prepare deliberately for a future Brain Bee attempt

## Study record

For each study session, record:

- Date
- Topic
- Resources used
- What I learned
- Questions I still have
- Practice score
- Topics to review

## Topic map

- Nervous system organization
- Neurons and glia
- Membrane potentials
- Synaptic transmission
- Neurotransmitters
- Sensory systems
- Motor systems
- Learning and memory
- Emotion and behavior
- Sleep
- Neurodevelopment
- Neurological disorders
- Brain imaging
- Experimental methods

## Mistake log

Keep a record of questions that were answered incorrectly and why the mistake happened. Revisit these topics during later review sessions.
