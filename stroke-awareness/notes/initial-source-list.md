# Initial Source List

These are the six sources currently being used for the stroke-awareness project. They should be cited in the paper according to the claims they support.

1. Wolters FJ, Paul NLM, Li L, Rothwell PM. **Sustained impact of UK FAST-test public education on response to stroke: a population-based time-series study.** International Journal of Stroke. 2015. DOI: 10.1111/ijs.12484. PMCID: PMC4672715.
   - Main relevance: FAST education, emergency response, time to seeking attention, hospital arrival, and public education.
   - Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4672715/

2. Mellon L, Doyle F, Rohde D, Williams D, Hickey A. **Stroke warning campaigns: delivering better patient outcomes? A systematic review.** Patient Preference and Adherence. 2015;6:61-73. DOI: 10.2147/PROM.S54087. PMCID: PMC4348144.
   - Main relevance: systematic review of public stroke education campaigns, prehospital delay, ambulance use, emergency department presentation, and thrombolysis.
   - Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4348144/

3. Tan S, Tang C, Ng JS, Ng C, Kovoor J, Gupta A, Goh R, Bacchi S, Jannes J, Kleinig T. **Delays in the diagnosis of ischaemic stroke presenting with persistent reduced level of consciousness: a systematic review.** Journal of Clinical Neuroscience. 2023;115:14-19. PMID: 37454440.
   - Main relevance: reduced level of consciousness (RLOC), diagnostic error/delay, and consequences of missed stroke.
   - Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37454440/

4. Cummings S, Kasner SE, Mullen M, Olsen A, McGarvey M, Weimer J, Jackson B, Desai N, Acker M, Messé SR. **Delays in the Identification and Assessment of in-Hospital Stroke Patients.** Journal of Stroke and Cerebrovascular Diseases. 2022;31(4):106327. DOI: 10.1016/j.jstrokecerebrovasdis.2022.106327.
   - Main relevance: delayed recognition and assessment of stroke occurring in hospital.
   - Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1052305722000246

5. Dolmans LSA, Hoes AW, Bartelink MLEL, Koenen NCT, Kappelle LJ, Rutten FH. **Patient delay in TIA: a systematic review.** Journal of Neurology. 2018/2019;266(5):1051-1058. DOI: 10.1007/s00415-018-8977-6. PMCID: PMC6469675.
   - Main relevance: delay after TIA, early risk of subsequent ischemic stroke, and the effect of rapid assessment/preventive treatment.
   - Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6469675/

6. Vatsalis T, Papadopoulos D, Georgousopoulou V, Bostantzis P, Rudolf J. **Global Awareness and Response to Early Symptoms of Acute Stroke: A Systematic Literature Review.** Cureus. 2025;17(2):e78420. DOI: 10.7759/cureus.78420. PMCID: PMC11802177.
   - Main relevance: public recognition of early stroke symptoms, response to symptoms, EMS activation, and awareness campaigns.
   - Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11802177/

## Verification note

The source titles and bibliographic details above were checked against PubMed/PMC or available bibliographic records. Individual numerical claims should still be checked against the relevant paper before being used in the final manuscript.
