# Stroke Awareness and Early Recognition

## Working topic

Early recognition and delayed recognition of stroke symptoms.

## Main areas of investigation

1. Why stroke is an emergency and the meaning of “time is brain”.
2. Why stroke symptoms may be missed or recognized late.
3. Public awareness of stroke warning signs and emergency response.
4. The practical benefits of early recognition and treatment.
5. Possible interventions such as education, awareness campaigns, and standardized assessment.

## Current status

Literature research and drafting.

## Important note

This is a student literature-based research project. Medical claims should be checked against the original research papers and authoritative clinical guidance before being included in the final version.

## Project files

- `notes/` — research notes
- `drafts/` — dated drafts of the paper
- `final/` — completed versions intended for archival or submission
