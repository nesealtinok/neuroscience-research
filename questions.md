# Questions I Want to Answer

A running list of neuroscience questions that may become future reading or research projects.

## Brain and neurological disease

- Why does a stroke cause weakness or sensory changes on one side of the body?
- Why can stroke symptoms be missed or recognized late?
- Why do some neurological conditions present with unusual or misleading symptoms?
- How does the brain recover after injury?
- What determines whether a neurological deficit becomes permanent?
